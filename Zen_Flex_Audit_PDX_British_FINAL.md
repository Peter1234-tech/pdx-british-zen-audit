## 2. Visibility

**Key Observations.** PDX British appears in local search results for Land Rover repair in Portland. They hold a claimed Yelp profile with 38 reviews and a 4.3-star average, and a Google Business Profile rated 4.8 stars across 40 reviews — strong signals for a niche specialist. However, the website is structurally thin, consisting of only three functioning pages (Home, Services, Testimonials), with no blog, no FAQ, and no individual service pages.

**Business Implication.** They are visible enough to be found by people who already know them or who are specifically searching for "PDX British." They are likely less visible to prospects searching broad terms like "Range Rover repair Portland" who are comparing multiple shops. Their minimal site structure limits organic search potential compared to competitors who are actively building out content.

**Evidence Gaps.** Specific local SEO rankings for high-intent keywords (e.g., "Range Rover repair Portland") were not fully mapped, but their minimal site structure suggests they are vulnerable to competitors with stronger SEO strategies.

## 3. Trust

**Key Observations.** The trust signals that exist are strong but poorly displayed. Google reviewers rate the shop 4.8 stars across 40 reviews, and Yelp reviewers repeatedly mention Francis Watson by name, praising his honesty and expertise across 16 separate reviews. The website testimonials page shows only five undated quotes in an accordion format with no links to third-party review sites like Google or Yelp. There are no staff photos, no bios, no certifications displayed, no before/after work gallery, and no warranty language. The BBB rating is A+, though the business is not formally accredited.

**Business Implication.** PDX British has immense real-world trust equity — a 4.8 Google rating, 30+ years of operation, and an owner personally praised across dozens of reviews — that is not being effectively translated to their digital presence. New visitors to the website do not immediately see the depth of their experience or the volume of their satisfied customers. The lack of team photos makes the business feel less personal online than it apparently is in person.

**Evidence Gaps.** It is not confirmed whether they hold ASE certifications or Land Rover-specific credentials that are simply not being advertised online.

## 4. Conversion

**Key Observations.** The website's conversion pathways are severely compromised. There is no phone number in the main header navigation. The primary "Contact Us" button in the navigation leads to a 404 error page. The "Schedule Service" buttons on the homepage do not lead to an online booking system. The only functional contact information — (503) 595-6600 and service@pdxbritish.com — is buried in the footer. There is no contact form, no live chat, and no clear "next step" guidance.

**Business Implication.** This is the most critical failure point for the business digitally. Prospects who are ready to book a service face immediate roadblocks. The broken contact page likely results in a high bounce rate for high-intent visitors. This friction forces customers to hunt for a phone number or abandon the site for a competitor with an easier booking process. A single lost repair job in this niche can represent $2,000–$10,000 in revenue.

**Evidence Gaps.** Without website analytics access, the bounce rate on the broken contact page cannot be quantified, but the structural failure is confirmed.

## 5. Response Handling

**Key Observations.** There are no visible systems for rapid response handling on the website. There is no web chat widget, no SMS/text-to-chat option, and no after-hours messaging protocol mentioned. One Yelp review explicitly complained about a lack of communication and missed appointments, while another noted that the shop is currently "slammed slammed slammed" and struggling with organization due to high volume.

**Business Implication.** The business appears to be at capacity and handling all communication manually. This creates a bottleneck where high-value leads are being lost to voicemail, unanswered emails, or callback delays. An AI front desk or automated booking system would directly address this without requiring additional staff. The current CTA — "(503) 595-6600 – Call or text to schedule" — is appropriate but unsupported by any digital infrastructure to catch overflow.

**Evidence Gaps.** We do not have access to their phone records or email response times, but customer reviews suggest communication delays are an active issue.

## 6. Follow-Up

**Key Observations.** There is no evidence of automated follow-up systems on the website. There are no lead magnets, newsletter signups, or service reminder opt-ins. The site does not suggest any systematic approach to nurturing leads or requesting reviews post-service. Despite 40 Google reviews and 38 Yelp reviews, there is no visible mechanism for systematically generating new reviews.

**Business Implication.** PDX British is almost certainly under-collecting Google reviews relative to the volume of satisfied customers they serve. A simple automated review request sent after each completed service could meaningfully increase their review count, further strengthening their 4.8-star Google profile. Additionally, deferred maintenance reminders and seasonal service prompts represent easy repeat revenue that is currently being left uncaptured.

**Evidence Gaps.** It is possible they use a shop management system (like Shop-Ware or Mitchell 1) internally that handles some email reminders, but nothing is integrated or promoted on the front-end website.

## 9. Vertical Lens Notes (Auto Repair / Land Rover Specialist)

In the specialized European auto repair niche, two factors dominate the customer decision: **technical credibility** and **trust**. Land Rover owners are acutely aware that their vehicles are complex and that poor repairs can be catastrophically expensive. They research shops carefully, read reviews, and look for signals of genuine expertise before committing.

PDX British has both the technical credibility (factory T4 TestBook diagnostic systems, 35 years of model-specific experience) and the trust (4.8 Google rating, loyal multi-year customers, owner personally praised by name). The problem is that none of this is effectively communicated online. A competitor with less experience but a better website will win the digital first impression, and in this niche, the digital first impression increasingly determines who gets the call.

**Evidence Gaps.** It is unclear if they service the newest models (2020+) with the same proficiency as the older classics, as their site leans heavily toward older models in its imagery.
