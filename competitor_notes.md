# Competitor Research Notes

## Competitor 1: Columbia Roving
- Website: columbiaroving.com (roverstest.com redirects)
- Address: 635 NW 16th Ave, Portland, OR 97209
- Phone: (503) 227-3766
- Yelp: 3.9 stars / 20 reviews (Unclaimed Yelp listing)
- Hours: Mon-Fri 8am-6pm
- Website: Very minimal/basic — appears to be a simple WordPress or similar template site. No phone number in nav. No online booking. COVID-19 notice still on site (outdated).
- Services: Land Rover specialist, also does customization/off-road outfitting
- Trust signals: Long-term customer reviews (10+ year customers mentioned), word of mouth strong
- Digital weaknesses: Unclaimed Yelp, minimal website, no booking system, outdated COVID notice
- Community reputation: Mentioned positively in Facebook community groups ("I don't recommend PDX British - I'd head to Columbia Roving first")
- Owner: Todd (mentioned in reviews)
- Note: Bryce Oliver (West Coast Overland founder) previously worked here

## Competitor 2: West Coast Overland
- Website: wcoverland.com
- Address: 607 NE Hancock Street, Portland, OR 97212
- Phone: 503-568-1808 (visible in nav header)
- Founded: 2021
- Services: Land Rover, Range Rover, Defender, Ineos Grenadier, Mercedes Sprinter — maintenance, repair, custom outfitting
- Website: Modern, professional Wix site. Phone number in nav. "BOOK ONLINE" in nav. Contact form. Staff photos and bios. Video content. Email newsletter signup. Social links (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
- Trust signals: Named staff with bios (Bryce Oliver, Mitchell Geyer - ASE Certified), testimonials on homepage, professional photography
- Digital strengths: Clear CTAs, phone in nav, online booking, modern design, team page, social presence
- Digital weaknesses: Newer business (est. 2021), fewer reviews than PDX British
- Key differentiator: Explicitly targets off-road/overland community + Sprinter vans (broader market)

## Competitor 3: Matrix Integrated (Portland + Bend)
- Website: matrixintegrated.cc
- Serves: Portland and Bend, OR
- Positions as: "premier dealership alternative for Land Rover repair in Portland and Bend Oregon"
- Family-owned, independent
- Note: Bryce Oliver (West Coast Overland) previously worked here too

## Summary Competitive Landscape
- PDX British: Most established (since 1991), most Yelp reviews (38), but weakest digital presence of the three
- West Coast Overland: Newest but strongest digital presence — phone in nav, online booking, staff bios, modern site
- Columbia Roving: Similar digital weakness to PDX British, unclaimed Yelp, minimal site
- PDX British's real-world reputation is strong but its digital presence significantly underrepresents it vs. newer competitors
