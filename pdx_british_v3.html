PDX British is a family-owned independent Land Rover and Range Rover repair shop founded in 1991 by Francis and Elsa Watson. We serve Portland, OR, and the broader Oregon and SW Washington market with honesty, expertise, and a genuine passion for these vehicles.

We use the factory Land Rover T4 TestBook diagnostic system, handle everything from oil changes to full engine rebuilds, and specialize in custom off-road outfitting. Our customers call us "the only shop they trust" – and we prove it every day.
