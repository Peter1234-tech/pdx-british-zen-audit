Our Story

Family-owned and operated since 1991. Portland's most trusted independent Land Rover specialists.

Since 1991

More Than Three Decades of Rover Expertise

PDX British was founded in 1991 by Francis Watson — a Land Rover enthusiast who turned his passion into Portland's most respected independent Rover shop. What started as a small operation has grown into a full-service specialist workshop serving Portland, Oregon and SW Washington.

Family owned since 1991, PDX British have taken care of thousands of happy Land Rover customers in Oregon and SW Washington. We strive to take care of your Land Rover as we would take care of ours… Experienced, fair and friendly service for all your Land Rover and Range Rover needs.

Francis and Elsa Watson are on-site every day. This isn't a franchise. It's not a chain. It's a family business built on the belief that Land Rover and Range Rover owners deserve honest, expert service without the dealer markup or the runaround.

We use the factory Land Rover T4 TestBook diagnostic system — the same tool franchised dealers use — giving us full access to every system in every Rover we work on. Our customers trust us with vehicles they've owned for decades, and we take that seriously.

Over the years, we've built a reputation in the Pacific Northwest Land Rover community that no advertising budget can buy: word of mouth from people who know their Rovers and know when they've found someone who actually understands them.
