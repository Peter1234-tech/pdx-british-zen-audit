Who We Are

Portland's Most Trusted Independent Rover Shop

PDX British has been Portland's go-to independent Land Rover and Range Rover specialist since 1991. We're not a dealership — we're enthusiasts who chose to make this our life's work.

  • Factory Land Rover T4 TestBook diagnostic system — the same tool dealers use
  • Full-service capability: oil changes through complete engine rebuilds
  • Custom off-road outfitting: lift kits, winches, bumpers, roof racks
  • Pre-purchase and for-sale inspections
  • EAS air suspension specialists & coil spring conversions
  • Military discount available · LGBTQ+ friendly shop
