## What Changed on the Website

The following improvements were made to the new design. **Nothing was changed on your current live site.**

**Functional fixes:**
- The contact form no longer shows a false "message sent" confirmation. It now shows a truthful message directing customers to call or email while the form backend is being connected.
- All external links (Google Reviews, Yelp, Google Maps) now open safely in new tabs with proper security settings.

**Conversion improvements:**
- A Quick Actions button row was added to the homepage and contact page, giving customers one-tap access to: Call Us, Directions, Google Reviews, Yelp Reviews, and Schedule Service.
- All phone numbers are clickable on mobile.
- All email addresses are clickable.

**Content improvements:**
- Service area language was added to the homepage, contact page, and footer: Portland, Lake Oswego, Beaverton, Tigard, Vancouver, SW Washington, and the broader Oregon Rover community.
- Three real named customer testimonials from your current site (Kev & Julia, Michelle, Barry) replaced the placeholder text on the homepage.
- Your business's own description from the current live site was added to the About page.
- "For-sale inspections" and "Mufflers" were added to the services menu to match your current site.
