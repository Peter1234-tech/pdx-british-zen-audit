## 3. Content Integrity Rules

- **No factual claims** (e.g., hours, phone numbers, services offered, certifications, awards, pricing) may be added, changed, or removed unless they are already present on the current site **or** have been explicitly verified and approved by the site owner.
- **No placeholder content** (e.g., "Lorem ipsum", dummy phone numbers, fake addresses) may be introduced at any point.
- **No third-party branding, logos, or endorsements** may be added without explicit owner approval.

---

## 4. Change Management Rules

All future changes to this website must follow these rules:

1. **Small and reversible** — Each change should be minimal in scope and easy to undo without affecting other parts of the site.
2. **Previewed before merging** — All changes must be reviewed and confirmed in a preview environment before being merged into `master`.
3. **No direct commits to `master`** — All work must be done on a feature branch and merged only after owner review.
4. **One concern per branch** — Each branch should address a single feature, fix, or improvement to keep changes clean and traceable.
